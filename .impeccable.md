# Design Context — Legal Document RAG Assistant

## Users
- General Counsel, Chief Compliance Officers, Legal Associates, and Enterprise Risk Auditors querying Vietnamese laws, decrees, and circulars.
- Working Context: High-stakes compliance verification, board advisory, cross-border investment audits, and contract due diligence.
- Job to be done: Retrieve exact statutory provisions with infallible citations, inspect exact wording in context, and produce verified legal memoranda.

## Brand Personality
- **Sovereign, Archival, Impeccable**
- 3-Word Voice: Authoritative, Architectural, Restrained.
- Emotional Goal: Unshakeable confidence in source provenance, calm clarity amidst legal complexity.

## Aesthetic Direction
- **Archetype**: *Archival Legal Intelligence & Codex Workbench*.
- **Color Discipline**:
  - Ink & Slate: Obsidian Deep (`#0B0F17`, `#111827`), Carbon (`#1F2937`), Slate Ink (`#334155`).
  - Warm Paper & Vellum: Alabaster (`#FBF9F5`), Natural Parchment (`#F4EFEA`), Bone Ledger (`#ECE6DC`).
  - Legal Brass & Seal: Antique Gilt (`#C59B27`), Warm Bronze (`#926B1E`), Muted Sage (`#3D7058`).
  - Hairline Rule: Warm Muted Border (`#E6E0D6`), Ink Border (`#232D3F`).
  - Strict BANS: No side-stripe `border-l-4` accents, no gradient text fills, no glowing cyan/purple bubbles, no identical 4-box card grids.
- **Typography Pairing**:
  - Display & Statutory Quotes: **Spectral** (Google Fonts — refined, dignified serif optimized for legislative & academic reading).
  - Interface & Navigation: **Albert Sans** (Google Fonts — crisp, contemporary, authoritative sans-serif).
  - Serial Numbers, Articles, Hashes: **JetBrains Mono** (monospace tabular numbers).

## Design Principles
1. **Dignity of the Codex**: Treat regulations with editorial gravitas. Use Spectral serif for statutory text, article headers, and exact quotations.
2. **Workbench Over Chatbot**: The Assistant is a structured research dossier with analytical findings, cited exhibits, and instant audit drawers — never casual chat bubbles.
3. **Asymmetric Information Density**: Replace repetitive metric cards with briefing ledgers, corpus status bars, and structured tables.
4. **Tactile Provenance**: Citations are styled as formal archival references with verified ledger seals, page folios, and instant side-by-side inspection.
